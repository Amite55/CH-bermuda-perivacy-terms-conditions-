# Privacy Policy
**Caring Hearts, operated by Caring Hearts Bermuda Ltd**  
*Last updated: October 7, 2026*

---

### 1. Introduction
Caring Hearts Bermuda Ltd ("we", "us", "our") operates the Caring Hearts mobile application (the "App") and the website [https://chbermuda.com/](https://chbermuda.com/). The App lets users book care and home services (transport, nursing, caregiving, home cleaning, in-home care, respite care and physiotherapy) from independent service providers and service provider companies. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices you have.

By using the App you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

---

### 2. Who can use the App
The App is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us at [info@chbermuda.com](mailto:info@chbermuda.com) and we will delete it.

---

### 3. Types of users
* **Users:** book and receive services.
* **Service providers:** offer services through the App.
* **Companies:** manage multiple providers and offer multiple services through the App.

---

### 4. Information we collect

#### a) Information you give us
* Name, email address, phone number, and password
* Address(es) and billing information
* Profile photo and other images you choose to upload from your gallery
* Booking details, service requests, and communications with us
* *We do not ask users to submit medical or health records through the App.*

#### b) Service provider profile information
Service providers and companies add details to their profile, such as their name, photo, the services they offer, and their areas of expertise or qualifications. This information is visible to users so they can choose a provider.

#### c) Location information
With your permission we collect your device's precise location only while the App is open (foreground). We use it so that your service provider can see your location and reach you. A provider cannot deliver a location-based service without it. You can turn off location permission in your device settings, but some features will not work.

#### d) Payment information
Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card details are entered into and handled by Stripe. We do not store your full card number. We receive limited payment details such as payment status, transaction ID, and the last four digits of the card.

#### e) Provider payouts
Providers and companies must set up a Stripe Connect account to receive payouts. During that setup, Stripe collects the identity and bank details it requires under its own privacy policy. We receive only your Stripe account ID and payout status, and we do not store your bank details.

#### f) Guest use
You can browse the App as a guest. An account is created only when you do something that needs one, such as making a booking.

---

### 5. How we use your information
* To create and manage your account and verify your email (OTP)
* To match you with providers and complete bookings
* To let providers locate you for a booking
* To process payments and provider payouts
* To respond to support requests
* To keep the App safe, prevent fraud, and fix problems
* To meet legal obligations

*We do not sell your personal information. We do not show advertising in the App.*

---

### 6. Who we share information with
* **Service providers and companies:** when you book, we share what is needed to deliver the service, such as your name, contact details, address, and location.
* **Stripe:** to process payments and provider payouts.
* **Google Maps:** to display maps and locations in the App.
* **Amazon Web Services (AWS):** our cloud hosting provider, which stores our database on our behalf.
* **Legal and safety:** if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of users, providers, or us.
* **Business transfer:** if our business is sold or merged, your information may be transferred to the new owner under this policy.

*We do not share your data with other third parties for advertising or marketing.*

---

### 7. Storage and security
Your data is stored in our database hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS) cloud servers. We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

---

### 8. Data retention and account deletion
We keep your information while your account is active and as needed to provide services and meet legal, tax, and accounting duties.

**How to delete your account:**
1. In the App, choose the delete account option and confirm with your password.
2. Our team will email you to confirm your request.
3. Once you confirm that you want to proceed, we delete your account. Your personal data is removed from our systems within 30 days.

You can also request deletion by emailing [info@chbermuda.com](mailto:info@chbermuda.com) from your registered email. Some records, such as payment and transaction records, may be kept longer where the law requires.

---

### 9. Your rights and choices
You can view and update your profile, address, and photo in the App. You can ask us to correct, delete, or give you a copy of your personal information, and you can withdraw location or gallery permission at any time in your device settings. Contact [info@chbermuda.com](mailto:info@chbermuda.com) and we will respond within a reasonable time. Where Bermuda's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) or other applicable law gives you additional rights, we will honour them.

* **Photos/Gallery:** to upload a profile image
