Privacy Policy

Operated by Caring Hearts Bermuda Ltd • Effective Date: October 7, 2026

1. Introduction

Caring Hearts Bermuda Ltd ("we", "us", "our") operates the Caring Hearts mobile application (the "App") and the website https://chbermuda.com/. The App lets users book care and home services from independent service providers and service provider companies across Bermuda.

Services Covered Under Caring Hearts

Our platform enables bookings for: transportation, nursing care, caregiving, home cleaning, in-home care, respite care, and physiotherapy.

This policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Who Can Use the App

The App is strictly intended for individuals aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18 years of age.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at info@chbermuda.com and we will take immediate steps to delete such data from our systems.

3. Types of Users

Users / Clients

Individuals who book and receive home care, transport, or nursing services.

Independent Providers

Independent professionals who offer care, nursing, or home services directly.

Service Provider Companies

Registered agencies managing multiple staff members and offering services.

4. Information We Collect

a) Information You Give Us

  • Account Credentials: Name, email address, phone number, and password.
  • Location & Billing: Service address(es) and billing details.
  • Profile Media: Profile photo and other images you choose to upload from your gallery.
  • Service Logs: Booking details, service requests, ratings, and communications with our support team.

Note: We do not ask users to submit medical or health records through the App.

b) Service Provider & Company Profile Information

Service providers and companies add details to their public profile, such as their full name, photo, services offered, rates, areas of expertise, and professional qualifications. This information is visible to users so they can make informed choices when booking a provider.

c) Location Information (Foreground Precise Location)

With your explicit permission, we collect your device's precise location only while the App is open (foreground). We use this so that your assigned service provider can see your destination/service location and navigate accurately to reach you. A provider cannot deliver a location-based service (such as transport or in-home care) without this access. You can turn off location permissions in your device settings at any time, but location-dependent features will cease to function.

d) Payment Information

Payments are processed securely via Stripe. Your credit/debit card details are entered directly into and handled by Stripe. We do not store your full card number or CVV on our servers. We receive only limited confirmation details such as payment status, transaction ID, card brand, and the last four digits of your card.

e) Provider Payouts (Stripe Connect)

Providers and companies must establish a Stripe Connect account to receive earnings. During setup, Stripe collects necessary identity verification and bank account details governed by Stripe’s Privacy Policy. Caring Hearts receives only your Stripe Account ID and payout status; we never store your raw bank details.

f) Guest Use

You may browse the App as a guest without creating an account. An account is generated only when you perform an action that requires identity verification, such as scheduling a booking.

5. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for specific, operational purposes:

  • To create and maintain your user or provider account and verify identity via One-Time Passwords (OTP).
  • To match clients with service providers and successfully complete service bookings.
  • To allow active service providers to locate your address when delivering services.
  • To process monetary transactions, invoices, and provider payouts via Stripe.
  • To provide customer support and respond to inquiries.
  • To ensure safety, prevent fraudulent activities, enforce terms, and resolve software issues.
  • To fulfill mandatory tax, legal, and regulatory requirements in Bermuda.
Zero Ad Guarantee

We do not sell your personal information. We do not serve third-party advertisements within the Caring Hearts App.

6. Who We Share Information With

We share data strictly with trusted third-party partners required to operate our service:

  • Service Providers and Companies: When a booking is initiated, we share essential fulfillment details (such as client name, contact number, service address, and live location) so the provider can perform the requested service.
  • Stripe: To process client credit card transactions and issue payouts to service providers.
  • Google Maps: To display interactive maps and facilitate location navigation within the App.
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS): Our cloud infrastructure host, storing our encrypted database on our behalf.
  • Legal & Safety Authorities: If required by Bermuda law, court order, or to protect the safety, rights, or property of users, providers, or Caring Hearts Bermuda Ltd.
  • Business Transfers: If Caring Hearts Bermuda Ltd undergoes a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, user data may be transferred under the terms of this privacy policy.

We do not share your personal data with any third party for marketing or advertising purposes.

7. Data Storage & Security

Your personal data is hosted on secure cloud servers provided by Amazon Web Services (AWS). We deploy industry-standard technical and organizational security measures including encrypted transport protocols (HTTPS/TLS), database encryption at rest, and strict access controls.

While we enforce rigorous safeguards, no electronic transmission or cloud system is 100% immune to security breaches. Consequently, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your personal information for as long as your account remains active or as required to deliver requested services, maintain tax records, and satisfy legal obligations under Bermuda law.

How to Delete Your Account
  1. In the App, choose the delete account option and confirm with your password.
  2. Our team will email you to confirm your request.
  3. Once you confirm that you want to proceed, we delete your account. Your personal data is removed from our systems within 30 days.

You can also request deletion by emailing info@chbermuda.com from your registered email. Some records, such as payment and transaction records, may be kept longer where the law requires.

9. Your Rights & Choices

You can view and update your profile, address, and photo in the App. You can ask us to correct, delete, or give you a copy of your personal information, and you can withdraw location or gallery permission at any time in your device settings. Contact info@chbermuda.com and we will respond within a reasonable time. Where Bermuda's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) or other applicable law gives you additional rights, we will honour them.

10. Mobile App Permissions

  • Location (while using the app): to show your location to your provider and for maps
  • Photos/Gallery: to upload a profile image

11. Third-Party Services

Stripe, Google Maps, and AWS have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for their practices.

12. International Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than where you live. We take steps to protect it wherever it is processed.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. We will post the new version here with a new "Last updated" date, and notify you in the App for significant changes.

You must be at least 18 years old to use the App. By using it, you confirm that you are 18 or over and that the information you provide is true and accurate.

4. Account Types

Guest

You can browse the App without an account. An account is needed to book a service or do other account-related activities.

User

Books and receives care, transport, nursing, or home services.

Service Provider

Company

5. Accurate Information

You must provide true, accurate, and current information. Giving fake or misleading information (including false user or provider details) is a breach of these Terms and may lead to suspension or removal of your account.

6. Service Providers and Verification

  • Providers and Companies offer only the services that our admin team has enabled for them.
  • Services that need a professional licence or registration, such as nursing and physiotherapy, are checked by us offline before they are enabled. Providers must hold all licences, qualifications, and permissions required by law and must keep them valid.
  • Providers set their own prices for each service.
  • We do our best to verify providers but do not guarantee the quality, safety, or suitability of any provider or service.

7. Booking Process & Reviews

  • A user requests a booking from a provider.
  • The provider may accept or reject the request.
  • A user can cancel a booking at any time until the provider accepts it. Once a provider has accepted a booking, the user can no longer cancel it in the App.
  • If a provider rejects a request, does not respond in time, or is late, our admin team may assign the booking to another provider.
  • If a provider cancels an accepted booking, the order is cancelled and the user is notified. The user can then book a new provider.
  • After the service, users can rate and review the provider. Reviews must be honest and must not contain abusive, false, or unlawful content.

8. Emergencies

EMERGENCY NOTICE

The App is not an emergency service. In a medical or life-threatening emergency, call your local emergency number first. If you need an urgent service through Caring Hearts, make the booking in the App first, then contact our admin team so they can arrange urgent help at info@chbermuda.com.

9. Prices and Payment

  • Prices are set by each provider for their services.
  • Users pay when they make the booking, and payments are processed by Stripe.
  • Caring Hearts does not charge a commission on bookings.
  • Providers and Companies receive payouts through Stripe Connect and must accept Stripe's terms.

10. Cancellation and Refunds

  • If a user cancels a booking before the provider accepts it, the amount paid is refunded in full. We do not charge a cancellation fee.
  • If a provider rejects or cancels a booking, the order is cancelled and the amount paid is refunded. The user can book another provider.
  • Refunds are returned to the original payment method through Stripe.
  • To ask about a refund or a cancelled booking, email info@chbermuda.com. Refund requests cannot currently be made inside the App.

Companies are responsible for the actions of every employee they assign.
